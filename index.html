Step 1

Scan your garden

Upload a clear photo that shows as much of your garden as possible. Daylight works best — stand back far enough to fit the whole bed, and include nearby trees if they cast shade.

Photo checklist

  • Taken in daylight, not at dusk
  • Shows the whole growing area
  • Soil surface is visible
  • Includes nearby trees and shade

Adding your garden details (sunlight, drainage, budget) makes the action plan more personal.
